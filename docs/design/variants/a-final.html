Three things you should decide before I write it

The typeface is the one real risk. Segoe UI Variable keeps the app native and free; bundling Inter (SIL OFL, roughly 400 KB for two static weights or one variable font) is what makes this read as Apple. Without it, A degrades to “a nice Windows card” and most of the difference disappears.

Green is new information. The current contract says the accent is for charging and active states only, and that low battery is a text cue. A adds a level colour to every rail, so the popup now communicates battery level twice — once as a number, once as a colour. That is the point of the widget idiom, but it is a contract change, not a restyle.

The shadow only holds while the composition is opaque. If the window ever becomes translucent or the OS draws it without a shadow, the card loses its edge and a 1 DIP hairline has to come back.

Still a design artifact, not a build: the DOM in this file is a reference, not WPF. Renders are vector-accurate at 100 % and CSS-scaled above that. On approval I rewrite POPUP.md, regenerate the four SVGs in popup/ and update preview.html so all three artefacts agree.
